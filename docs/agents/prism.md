# Prism / Queen Raida

**Prism** is RaidGuild’s organizational agent system. In Discord it commonly appears as **Queen Raida**. Its purpose is to help the Guild turn ongoing activity into durable memory, reusable workflows, and useful tools without flattening context or ignoring permissions.

## What Prism Connects

- **Knowledge sources** — approved handbook pages, living documents, meeting notes, code, and other scoped sources.
- **Durable memory** — summaries and structured records that preserve decisions, context, and open threads over time.
- **Shared workflows** — repeatable content, operations, code, and research tasks.
- **Surfaces** — Discord, chat, apps, and tools used by members and stewards.

## Permissions Are Contextual

Prism does not have one universal level of access.

- A channel may expose only a read-only slice of the knowledge base.
- A workflow may allow the agent to create or update a specific kind of record.
- Steward categories may use agent profiles tailored to their responsibilities.
- Future member profiles may have different sources and actions.

Always check the visible workflow and audience before sharing sensitive material or asking the agent to act. Agent output should preserve source references when it summarizes Guild knowledge.

## Intended Uses

- preparing and organizing meeting notes;
- retrieving Guild knowledge with references;
- drafting content and operational artifacts;
- supporting steward workflows and recurring coordination;
- generating and reviewing code; and
- helping turn activity into daily, weekly, and topic-level living documents.

## Human Responsibility

Prism supports judgment; it does not replace it. Members remain responsible for checking sensitive claims, governance proposals, client commitments, financial instructions, permissions, and anything that acts outside a reversible draft workflow.

Do not treat an agent response as ratified Guild policy. The handbook, final RIPs, onchain proposals, contracts, and decisions recorded by the responsible people remain the durable authorities for their respective domains.

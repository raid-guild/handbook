# DAO Shares, xDAI, and wxDAI

RaidGuild membership is represented by **DAO shares**, not the RAID token. New members do not need to acquire or stake RAID as part of the current membership process.

## The Assets You Need to Know

| Asset | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **DAO shares** | Non-transferable governance power and a proportional ragequit claim on eligible Guild treasury assets. One share carries one vote. |
| **Loot** | A non-voting economic claim that may be eligible for ragequit, depending on the DAO’s current configuration. |
| **xDAI** | The native stable asset used for gas and payments on Gnosis Chain. |
| **wxDAI** | Wrapped xDAI used by DAO contracts and the current new-member contribution. |

:::warning Review note · Confirm before execution

The August 2026 steward walkthrough confirmed a new-member package of **500 wxDAI for 100 shares**. Check current DAO settings before acting; parameters and user interfaces can change.

:::

## Prepare a Gnosis Chain Wallet

Use a wallet you control and add Gnosis Chain through your wallet’s trusted network directory or the [official Gnosis Chain documentation](https://docs.gnosischain.com/about/networks/gnosis). Keep a small amount of xDAI available for gas.

## Acquire and Wrap xDAI

Acquire xDAI through a reputable on-ramp, exchange withdrawal, or bridge that currently supports Gnosis Chain. Confirm the route, destination chain, token contract, fees, and wallet address before sending funds.

To convert xDAI to wxDAI, use a current Gnosis-compatible interface and verify the canonical wxDAI contract:

[`0xe91D153E0b41518A2Ce8Dd3D7944Fa863463a97d`](https://gnosisscan.io/token/0xe91D153E0b41518A2Ce8Dd3D7944Fa863463a97d)

:::warning Verify the live route

Bridge and swap interfaces change frequently. Do not follow an old Polygon, Connext, Honeyswap, or WrapETH click path from a static handbook without checking current official documentation and contract addresses.

:::

## RAID Is Legacy Context

RAID exists historically, and older proposals or articles may mention staking and Champion rewards. Those mechanics are not part of the current membership path. Consult original governance records when researching that history; do not treat them as present-day onboarding instructions.

# Brand and Design System

RaidGuild’s visual identity evolves through named **brand steward reigns**. The black crossed-swords mark is the durable master mark; palettes, typography, illustration, motion, and voice treatments belong to a particular reign rather than timeless canon.

The current development direction is **Louchi — Venture Beyond**: deep teal and cyan atmosphere, parchment surfaces, coral signals, acid-lime highlights, expansive editorial type, and Moebius-influenced speculative-world imagery.

## Applying the System

1. Preserve a recognizable crossed-swords mark and a route back to RaidGuild.org.
2. Name the reign when presenting visual guidance; do not claim one steward era is timeless.
3. Use semantic tokens for background, foreground, action, border, and focus states.
4. Use Mazius Display for display moments, EB Garamond for body copy, and Ubuntu Mono for metadata, labels, dates, and technical identifiers.
5. Support day and night appearances without changing information architecture.
6. Preserve keyboard focus, contrast, touch access, and `prefers-reduced-motion` behavior.
7. When representing a specialized practice, retain Guild provenance with the crossed-swords mark, “A RaidGuild practice,” and “Operating through RaidGuild LLC” where the legal relationship matters.

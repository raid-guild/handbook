**Discord Etiquette: Navigating the Citadel**

Welcome to the RaidGuild Discord, our bustling citadel and central hub for collaboration, planning, and discussion. To keep our space constructive and welcoming for all members, we follow a few key guidelines. By observing these, you’ll help maintain an environment that is efficient, respectful, and enjoyable for everyone.

Here’s what to keep in mind when joining the conversation:

1. **Avoid Spamming Channels**  
   Refrain from posting the same message in multiple channels. Keep your posts relevant and purposeful, and ensure each message adds value to the conversation.

2. **Limit Member Mentions**  
   Use `@RaidGuildMember` tags sparingly—limit to once per week unless an urgent need arises. This ensures our notifications stay meaningful and our members remain focused.

3. **Respect Member-Only Meetings**  
   Only join private, member-only meetings if you’ve been explicitly invited. Respecting these boundaries helps keep our gatherings efficient and organized.

4. **Post in Relevant Channels**  
   Take a moment to ensure your post lands in the most suitable channel. This keeps information organized and makes it easier for everyone to find what they’re looking for.

5. **Engage Constructively and Respectfully**  
   Approach every conversation with respect and an open mind. Debate and differing opinions are part of healthy collaboration—focus on solutions and constructive feedback, even when you disagree.

By following these guidelines, you’ll be contributing to a positive, productive Discord environment where everyone can do their best work.

**Discord Navigation: Your Guide to the Guild’s Channels**

Welcome to the RaidGuild Discord server, where Guilders gather, plan, and share in all things Web3. Our server is divided into channels for different purposes, from casual conversations to mission-critical projects. Here’s a guide to help you find your way and connect with the right Guilders.

If you’re ever unsure about where to post, simply ask in **#tavern** (for Apprentices) or **#general** (for Guild Members). For additional guidance, tag `@Tavern Keeper` for support.

---

### Server Categories and Key Channels

Our server is organized into several categories, each with channels that serve specific functions. Below is a breakdown of these categories and what you’ll find in each.

---

#### **Public Channels**
Accessible to all members, these channels foster connection, creativity, and conversation between Guilders and Apprentices.

#### **Village**
A collaborative area for brainstorming, resources, and sharing projects.

#### **Cohort Seasons (n)**
Seasons are structured onboarding cohorts, helping new Apprentices integrate and learn together.

#### **Guild Commons**
Reserved for member-exclusive conversations about Guild strategy, treasury, and community growth.

#### **Guild HQ**
This is the heart of RaidGuild operations, where Guilders discuss internal improvements, client projects, and raid coordination.

#### **Internal Services**
For managing and improving internal tools, resources, and processes.

#### **House of Memes**
Focused on RaidGuild’s external presence, marketing, and shilling strategies.

#### **Machine Shop**
Home to our Discord bots, including tools for member availability and server maintenance.

#### **Active Raids/RIPs**
Dedicated channels for each live Raid and RIP (Raid Improvement Proposal), marked by `#raid-project` or `#rip-project`. Each raid has a pinned message explaining its scope.

If you’re interested in contributing to a Raid, visit the specific channel and introduce yourself, offering any skills that could add value.

---

#### **Client Chats**
Temporary channels for client discussions while scoping a project. These spaces allow you to get to know the client’s needs and preferences. Respect client confidentiality, as misuse of this information can lead to removal from the Guild.

---

#### **Valhalla Closed Raids**
Where completed and archived Raids are sent once shipped. For more on closing Raids, see the **Ship a Raid** section.

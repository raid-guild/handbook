# Become an Apprentice or Cohort Graduate

:::warning Review note

RaidGuild currently uses both **cohort graduate** and **apprentice** for the stage between a successful cohort demo and full DAO membership. This draft keeps both terms until the Guild settles on one public name.

:::

## Apply Through Portal

1. Create or update your [Portal profile](https://portal.raidguild.org).
2. Signal interest in the next cohort through Portal.
3. Join Discord and follow the current instructions in Castle Walls.
4. Use Portal for cohort dates, sessions, and schedule changes.

## What the Cohort Includes

The cohort combines a concentrated onboarding period with knowledge sharing and workshops across the month. It is designed to help you understand the Guild, meet other builders, and turn what you learn into a concrete demo.

**Demo Day is the gate from cohort participant to cohort graduate / apprentice.** A cohort host’s approval of the demo is not the same as championing someone for full membership.

## What Comes Next

After graduating, you can begin building the trust and body of work needed for membership:

- contribute to a client Raid or internal RIP;
- attend Raider Roundup, cohort calls, and other sessions available to you;
- use selected Agency spaces to find work and collaborators;
- learn the Guild’s values and demonstrate them under real conditions; and
- develop a relationship with a current member who could later Champion you.

There is no automatic promotion. Graduation opens the next part of the path; contribution, craft, communication, and values alignment show that you are ready for full membership.

:::note Paid work

Graduates may contribute to paid Raids, but payment requires registration with RaidGuild LLC and completion of its light KYC process.

:::

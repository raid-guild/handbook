# Champion a New Member

Championing is a public act of trust. A Champion tells the membership why a cohort graduate / apprentice is ready, helps surface questions, and sponsors the onchain membership proposal.

The current process does **not** require staking RAID tokens and does not include a RAID reward.

## Two Different Vouches

1. **Cohort approval** — a cohort host gives a thumbs-up to the participant’s Demo Day work and confirms graduation.
2. **Membership Champion** — a different current member vouches for full membership after seeing the graduate contribute and live the Guild’s values.

Keeping these decisions separate gives the wider membership evidence beyond a single cohort experience.

## How to Champion Someone

1. **Know their work.** Be able to speak concretely about their contribution to a Raid, RIP, or other Guild activity.
2. **Discuss expectations.** Confirm that the candidate understands governance, the 500 wxDAI / 100 share membership package, and the difference between DAO membership and LLC payroll registration.
3. **Announce the relationship at Raider Roundtable.** Explain why you are willing to Champion the candidate and invite questions from members.
4. **Socialize the proposal.** Give members enough time and context to evaluate the candidate before the onchain vote.
5. **Sponsor the membership proposal.** Help the candidate prepare an accurate proposal and follow it through processing.
6. **Stay available.** Championing is an ongoing relationship, not a transaction receipt.

:::warning Review note · Confirm the package

The August 2026 audit confirmed a 500 wxDAI contribution for a new-member cap of 100 shares. Verify current DAO settings before the proposal is submitted.

:::

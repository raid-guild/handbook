# Social Media: Connect with the Guild

The Guild extends its presence beyond our digital halls! Follow RaidGuild across the dark forest to stay up-to-date, connect with fellow raiders, and dive into our latest adventures in the Ethereum ecosystem. Here’s where we share updates, insights, and the stories behind our Raids—come join us!

- **[Discord](https://discord.gg/rGFpfQf)**  
  Step into our official Guild hall! This is where RaidGuild members gather, discuss projects, plan raids, and share knowledge. It’s the best place to connect with the community and get involved.

- **[GitHub](https://github.com/raid-guild/)**  
  Check out our open-source projects, code, and collaborations.

- **[Twitter](https://twitter.com/RaidGuild)** - @RaidGuild  
  Follow us on Twitter for real-time updates, project highlights, and thoughts from the Guild. Jump into the conversation with #RaidGuild and be part of our journey to build a decentralized world.

- **[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/raid-guild/mycompany/)**  
  Stay connected with RaidGuild’s professional side on LinkedIn. See our latest ventures, client partnerships, and join us in showcasing the power of DAOs in the Web3 industry.

- **Newsletter** - [Substack](https://raidguild.substack.com/) & [Medium](https://medium.com/raid-guild)  
  Prefer to stay in the loop through storytelling and insights? Our Substack and Medium are home to articles, case studies, and deep dives into the work we do and the principles we stand by.

Explore, follow, and connect! These are your portals to stay engaged with the Guild as we work to shape the decentralized future, one raid at a time.

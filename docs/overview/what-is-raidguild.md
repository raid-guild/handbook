# What Is RaidGuild?

RaidGuild is a collective of Web3 builders, code warriors, and visionaries united under a shared mission: to create and protect open, decentralized systems in the Ethereum ecosystem. As a community-driven DAO, we work to forge the tools, infrastructure, and experiences that empower a trustless, permissionless future. RaidGuild's strength lies in our members' skills and their dedication to a fairer, decentralized world, and we channel that power through every project, partnership, and initiative.

### Who We Are

We are a fellowship of designers, developers, strategists, and technologists who thrive on the frontlines of Web3. RaidGuild is a decentralized organization that operates without a single leader, where each member plays a critical role in defining our culture, shaping our strategies, and executing Raids. Our guild model encourages collaboration and self-governance, empowering members to share knowledge and hone their craft.

At our core, RaidGuild is a meritocratic community. Members advance by proving their skills, learning from one another, and taking on responsibilities that align with their strengths. We embrace a spirit of growth, pushing each member to reach their potential while collectively working towards a fairer, decentralized future.

### What We Do

We are architects and protectors of the Ethereum ecosystem. RaidGuild provides end-to-end services to Web3 projects—from design and development to strategy and deployment. Our members specialize in building decentralized applications, smart contract systems, DAOs, and supporting Web3 tools. Every project, or “Raid,” is approached with our commitment to quality and collaboration, drawing from a pool of Guild members with complementary expertise.

Our Raids serve both emerging projects and established organizations, helping them to build decentralized systems that prioritize user sovereignty, security, and transparency. Our collective efforts are about more than single projects—they are part of a broader mission to advance the potential of Web3, one Raid at a time.

### What We Believe

RaidGuild’s values are rooted in decentralization, transparency, and fairness. We believe in an Ethereum ecosystem where users have control over their assets and data, projects operate without centralized gatekeepers, and systems are resilient to manipulation. To uphold these ideals, we champion:

- **Open-Source Development**: We believe that knowledge should be shared and accessible, pushing forward innovation for the public good.
- **User Sovereignty**: Protecting user freedom and privacy is at the core of our mission; we build systems that give users the power to control their data, assets, and digital interactions.
- **Trustlessness**: We rely on code, not centralized entities, to establish trust, ensuring transparency and security within the ecosystem.

These principles guide every decision, partnership, and execution at RaidGuild. Through them, we uphold the vision of a decentralized future and contribute to the evolution of Ethereum and Web3.

**RaidGuild is more than a service provider—it’s a movement.** Through our work, we aim to protect, strengthen, and expand the Ethereum ecosystem, transforming Web3 ideals into reality.

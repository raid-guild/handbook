# What Is RaidGuild?

RaidGuild is a builder-owned collective for people working in Web3, applied AI, and other frontier fields. We combine the trust and fellowship of a long-running community with the ability to assemble focused teams around difficult problems.

We are not a conventional agency org chart. Designers, developers, researchers, strategists, operators, and technologists shape the Guild through contribution, reputation, and DAO governance. Client projects are called **Raids**; the people doing the work form a **Raid Party**.

## How We Work

RaidGuild is the center of gravity: the community, talent pool, shared reputation, contracts, treasury access, and operating knowledge. Specialized practices—our **Tips of the Spear**—develop deeper expertise while remaining part of that shared network.

Current practices include:

- **Applied AI**, focused on agents, workflows, interfaces, and forward-deployed AI systems.
- **Onchain / Web3 solutions**, carrying forward the Guild’s deep experience with decentralized systems.
- **Placement services**, connecting members with external roles through the network.

:::warning Review note

Placement is still a developing service. Its operating model, ownership, and fee require Guild review before this description becomes settled handbook guidance.

:::

Every practice may have its own positioning and visual expression, but no single practice is the whole Guild.

## What We Believe

- **Build in the open when we can.** Shared knowledge and open-source work compound community capability.
- **Protect agency.** We design systems that give people meaningful control over their work, data, assets, and decisions.
- **Earn trust through craft.** Clear communication, reliable delivery, and accountable follow-through matter as much as technical ambition.
- **Learn together.** The Guild grows when members share context, mentor one another, and turn project experience into durable knowledge.
- **Resist Moloch.** We organize against coordination failure, extraction, and the forces that make good collective work harder.

RaidGuild is more than a service provider. It is a fellowship for building at the frontier—and an operating system for turning trusted relationships into excellent work.

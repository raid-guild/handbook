## Why Raid on Optimism?

RaidGuild has lived on Gnosis chain for over 3 years, so why change chains now? There are medium-sized reasons: Optimism has better on/off-ramps, more liquidity, more 3rd-party providers and tooling, stronger builder community, and a stronger and more aligned vision. By simply considering the user experience for contractors and clients on Optimism vs Gnosis, the benefit of switching seems obvious. However, there is a larger reason why members should consider raiding on Optimism: **it is being used to fund a new and clearer legal structure for our DAO.**

As part of a [long-term RIP to improve RaidGuild's legal standing](https://github.com/raid-guild/RIPs/issues/165), a DAO has been created on Optimism specifically for collecting spoils from Optimism raids and using that revenue to fund the formation of much needed financial and legal web2 interfaces. This could include a new umbrella non-profit entity, a tradfi bank account, product-focused spin-off entities, web2 billing rails, or anything else that comes up in the future. The reason for keeping this funding on a separate chain and in a separate DAO is to keep bookkeeping separate, and to keep the initiative opt-in.

By raiding on Optimism, you are supporting an initiative for RaidGuild to play nicer with web2 institutions. This means we can receive grant money with greater ease, provide legal resources and protection for all members, create clearer and stronger agreements with other members and members, and start spinning off product DAOs that can give back to the guild without legal ambiguity.

While the funding (via raids) of this initiative begins now, the bulk of the initiative will unfold throughout the begining of 2025. At any point, if raiding on Optimism or forming a new legal entity no longer makes sense, the RIP with be spun-down and completely reverted.
